# Shipping and Delivery — Acme Outfitters

## Shipping options
| Option    | Delivery time         | Price                                    |
|-----------|-----------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Standard  | 3–6 business days     | $5.95, free on orders over $75           |
| Express   | 1–2 business days     | $14.95                                   |
| Overnight | next business day     | $29.95, order before 1 pm ET             |

Gold and Platinum members get free Express shipping on every order.

## Carriers
We ship with UPS, FedEx and DHL. The carrier is chosen automatically based on destination and package size and
cannot be changed after the order ships.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
If tracking has not updated for **5 business days**, contact support and we open a trace with the carrier.

## Lost or stolen parcels
If a parcel is marked delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbours and wait 48 hours — carriers
sometimes mark parcels delivered early. After 48 hours we open a claim and send a replacement or refund once the
carrier confirms the loss (typically 3–5 business days).

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the UK and the EU. Duties and taxes are collected at checkout, so there are no surprise fees
on delivery. International returns are not eligible for free return labels.

## Address changes
The shipping address can be changed while an order is PROCESSING. Once SHIPPED, address changes must be requested
through the carrier directly.
