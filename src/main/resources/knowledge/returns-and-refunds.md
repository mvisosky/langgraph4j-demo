# Returns and Refunds Policy — Acme Outfitters

## Return window
Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method.
Platinum members get an extended **60-day** return window. The window is measured from the delivery date shown in
order tracking, not the order date.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached. Footwear must not show outdoor wear on the soles.
Tents and sleeping bags may be returned if they were set up indoors only.

## Final-sale items
Items marked **final sale** (sample sales, clearance below 50% off, personalised products) cannot be returned or
refunded unless they arrive damaged or defective. Damaged final-sale items are handled under the warranty policy.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, report it within **7 days of delivery**. We refund or
replace it at no cost, including return shipping, and you do not need to send the item back if it is under $25.

## How refunds are processed
- Refunds up to **$100** are approved automatically once eligibility is confirmed.
- Refunds above $100 are reviewed by a support specialist, usually within one business day.
- Approved refunds reach the original payment method in **5–10 business days** depending on the bank.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

## Orders not yet delivered
Orders in PROCESSING can be cancelled free of charge. Orders already SHIPPED cannot be cancelled; refuse the
delivery or return the parcel after it arrives.

## Return shipping
Return labels are free for Gold and Platinum members. Standard members pay a flat $6.95 return fee deducted from
the refund, except when the return is due to our error.

## How to return an item
1. Pack the item in its original packaging with tags attached, and include your order number (a note is fine if you
   no longer have the packing slip).
2. Get your return label. Gold and Platinum members receive a prepaid label by email once the refund is approved.
   Standard members can print one from **My Account > Orders > Return item**; the $6.95 return fee is deducted from
   the refund.
3. Attach the label and drop the parcel at any carrier location. No appointment is needed.
4. Send it within **14 days of the refund being approved**, and keep the carrier receipt until the refund appears on
   your statement.

## Does the refund depend on sending the item back?
Refunds are issued as soon as they are approved: you do not have to wait for us to receive the item. In return we
expect the item to be sent back within 14 days of approval. If a returned item has not reached us within 30 days of
approval, or arrives in a condition that breaks the rules above (for example used footwear), we may reverse the
refund. The exception is damaged or wrong items under $25, which you can keep, and items we ask you to dispose of.
