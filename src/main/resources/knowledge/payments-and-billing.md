# Payments and Billing — Acme Outfitters

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Acme gift cards. We do not accept cheques
or cash on delivery.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. Split shipments are charged
separately, so you may see more than one charge for one order.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within **14 days** of purchase, we refund the difference once per item.
Price adjustments do not apply to clearance or flash-sale prices.

## Duplicate or unrecognised charges
Pending authorisations from cancelled orders disappear within 3–5 business days. If you see a charge you do not
recognise, contact support: billing disputes are always escalated to a billing specialist.

## Security
Support agents will **never** ask for your full card number, CVV or password. Never share them in chat.
