# Product Care Guide — Acme Outfitters

## Waterproof jackets
Wash in cold water with a technical cleaner, no fabric softener. Tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to reactivate
the water-repellent (DWR) finish. Re-proof with a spray-on DWR treatment once or twice a season.

## Down jackets and sleeping bags
Wash with a down-specific detergent in a front-loading machine. Dry on **low** heat with two clean tennis balls
until completely dry, which can take several hours. Store loosely, never compressed.

## Hiking boots
Remove mud with a soft brush after every hike. Let boots air-dry away from direct heat — radiators and fires
crack leather and weaken glue. Re-waterproof leather with wax every few months.

## Tents
Always dry the tent completely before storage to prevent mildew. Clean with a sponge and cold water, never in a
washing machine. Store loosely in a breathable bag.

## Merino wool
Machine wash cold on a wool cycle and lay flat to dry. Merino resists odour, so it needs washing less often than
synthetic fabrics.
