# Warranty — Acme Outfitters

## Coverage
All Acme-branded gear carries a **2-year warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship. Tents, packs and
outerwear carry a **lifetime warranty** on seams and zippers.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, misuse, improper care (for example machine-drying a down jacket on
high heat), and modifications are not covered. Third-party brands are covered by their own manufacturer warranty.

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Contact support with your order number and photos showing the defect.
2. A specialist reviews the claim within 3 business days.
3. If approved, we repair, replace or issue store credit at our discretion. Repairs typically take 2–3 weeks.

Warranty claims always require human review and are handled as support tickets.

## Proof of purchase
Orders placed online are linked to your account, so no receipt is needed. For in-store purchases please provide
the receipt or the card statement showing the purchase.
