# Acme Trail Club — Loyalty Program

## Tiers
| Tier     | How to qualify              | Benefits                                                                |
|----------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Standard | Create an account           | 1 point per $1, member-only sales                                        |
| Gold     | $500 spent in 12 months     | 1.5 points per $1, free Express shipping, free return labels             |
| Platinum | $1,500 spent in 12 months   | 2 points per $1, free Express shipping, free returns, 60-day returns, priority support |

## Points
100 points = $5 in rewards. Rewards are issued in $5 increments and expire 12 months after they are issued.
Points are earned on the merchandise total after discounts, excluding taxes and shipping. Points from a refunded
order are deducted.

## Tier status
Tier is re-evaluated monthly based on the previous 12 months of spending. Members keep their tier for at least
12 months after qualifying.

## Birthday reward
Gold and Platinum members get a $15 birthday reward, valid for 30 days.
